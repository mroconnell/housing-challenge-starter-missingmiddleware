# Housing Data and Technology Innovation Challenge Official Data Source List

The following table provides information on GIS/Open Data portals for Arizona, Connecticut, Oregon, Hawaii, Pennsylvania, Tennessee, Utah, Virginia, Wisconsin, Rhode Island, and New Hampshire. It contains links to various parcel, zoning, and land-use/cover datasets for each state, where available. The list is not comprehensive but serves as a foundational body of knowledge to use as a starting point for the Housing Data and Technology Innovation Challenge. Participants are not limited to these data sources; they may incorporate any other data sets as they see fit, provided they adhere to the data-inclusion policies defined in Section 7(b)(iii) of the [Official Rules](https://housingchallenge.turnout.rocks/rules.html).

Use Census counts at their published geography. Any allocation to individual parcels must be labeled as estimated and tested for sensitivity. Do not infer annual construction by subtracting overlapping ACS five-year releases; follow [Census comparison guidance](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/guidance/comparing-acs-data.html). B25034 describes the age of surviving housing in year bands, not annual construction events.

The supplied statewide zoning downloads are county-administered layers from 2016–2017, not a current municipal zoning baseline. The linked Wiscland 2 land-cover product is a 2016 release based primarily on 2010–2014 imagery, with 30-metre pixels and a two-acre minimum mapping unit; use it only for suitable broad context, not current backyard or parking-lot identification. See the [zoning directory](https://www.sco.wisc.edu/data/zoning/) and [DNR service metadata](https://dnrmaps.wi.gov/arcgis_image/rest/services/DW_Land_Cover/EN_Land_Cover2_Lev2/MapServer).

Local Wisconsin zoning, assessor, and permit records will supplement the Census baseline where accessible. Agency transit schedules and walking-network data will support TOD access measures. Utah's [Housing Unit Inventory](https://gis.utah.gov/products/sgid/planning/housing-unit-inventory/) and California's [HCD APR data](https://www.hcd.ca.gov/apr) are candidate comparison sources, with coverage, definitions, and transfer assumptions to audit before use.

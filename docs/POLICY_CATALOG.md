# Policy Catalog

Created and last updated: 2026-10-23.

This is the initial California ADU legislation register for the housing-policy MVP. It combines the California YIMBY report supplied by the user, the Mercatus index, and the previously prioritized 2020 exemption reform. It is not an exhaustive catalog through 2026 or a completed set of model rules. Wisconsin local-policy records will be added after the Madison and New Berlin audits; Menlo Park is a California fee-history research candidate.

See [AQ-013 through AQ-017](ASSUMPTIONS_AND_QUESTIONS.md#aq-013-center-california-calibration-on-the-january-2020-exemption-reform) for the research decisions, policy-date conventions, and historical permit sources.

## Read this register

- The legislative year identifies the bill, not the start of its effects. Exact legislative passage and signing dates remain unentered unless explicitly recorded below; they must not be inferred from the year.
- An effective date and an operative provision or implementation deadline may differ. Use the date applicable to the provision being modeled.
- **Agency-supported** means an official agency memo or handbook supports the broad summary and dates. It does not mean every clause or historical local application has been coded. **Source lead** means further primary-source verification is needed.
- Where bills form a package, the summary describes the package. Verify chaptering and clause attribution before assigning an individual bill a separate model effect. No effect sizes are assigned in this register.

## Records to develop from the register

For each model-relevant provision, add: stable provision ID; parent bill IDs; jurisdiction ID and scope; historical code section/version; prior and new rule; eligible building/parcel types; ADU form; size/count limits; setbacks/height; parking/replacement parking; approval process; occupancy/rental limits; applicable fees; exclusions; exact effective-from/effective-to dates; operative or implementation dates; primary-source URL and page/section; retrieval date; verification status; and unresolved interpretation.

Use historical versions instead of applying the current handbook backward. The exemption pathway's historical citation is section 65852.2(e), later section 66323; a renumbering alone is not a production treatment. State authorization, local implementation, and additional rights beyond the local baseline must remain separate.

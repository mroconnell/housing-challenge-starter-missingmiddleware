## Left out on purpose
- LAX Automated People Mover: an airport people mover, not one of the modes in scope.
- Renames with no move (e.g. SD Alvarado Medical Center to Alvarado (2023-12) to UC San Diego Health East (2024-09)).
- Temporary suspensions (SacRT Green Line 2025-06 to 2026-10-12; Caltrain Hillsdale 2020-05 to 2021-04 is in the CSV as a relocation).

# Cleaning and Preparation

- **Census foundation** ([details](gate2/FOUNDATION.md)): keep Wisconsin rows from the national ACS files; join ACS to TIGER 2024 by GEOID (all 1,925 county subdivisions, 1,542 tracts and 4,692 block groups match); compute margins of error for derived sums; crosswalk BPS jurisdictions to municipalities (95.2% direct match in 2025, the rest to county unincorporated rows).
- **Madison permits** ([details](gate2/MADISON_PERMITS.md)): keep rows whose permit number matches `^BLD[A-Z]{3}-\d{4}-\d+$`; drop owner and contractor columns at ingestion; drop duplicate reissued permits; classify ADUs from description keywords; match street addresses to city address points to reach parcels.
- **Pleasant Prairie and Sun Prairie permits** ([details](gate2/COMPARISON_PERMITS.md)): calculate units from permit type and description; remove owner and builder names from descriptions; give withdrawn, void and denied Sun Prairie records 0 units; match parcel numbers to V12 PARCELID; read a prior dwelling from the parcel's earlier permits; place parcels in tracts and block groups by point-in-polygon against TIGER 2024.
- **California 2012–2019 series** ([AQ-017](ASSUMPTIONS_AND_QUESTIONS.md)): statewide totals are team sums of the 527 published jurisdiction rows. For 2018 onward, prefer HCD APR data and keep both versions.
- **Gate 3 sources** (statewide-law states, five cities, Madison-like cities, Wisconsin measured places, town zoning, California rule sources): cleaning rules are in each method doc in [docs/gate3/](gate3/README.md) (STATEWIDE_STATES §3 and §6, OTHER_CITIES §3, WI_RULES_VS_ACTIVITY, WI_BASELINE, WI_UNZONED_TOWNS, CA_RULES_VS_ACTIVITY, MADISON_LIKE_CITIES). Statewide-state counts are transcribed or extracted from the official files into `SERIES` in `missing_middleware/comparison/statewide_states.py`; the build does not re-read those files.

# Known Limitations

- 724 Wisconsin towns (22% of one-unit detached homes) have no BPS row of their own; their permits appear only in county unincorporated rows.
- ACS 5-year estimates average 2020–2024 and carry margins of error. PEP housing-unit estimates measure net stock change, not new construction.
- Madison permits have no ADU flag, no application or final dates, and free-text unit suffixes, so ADU counts depend on the keyword rules.
- Pleasant Prairie and Sun Prairie have no dwelling-unit or year-built field, so a prior dwelling is inferred from permit history. Sun Prairie has submission dates only, and its counts run 14–29% below BPS in 2016, 2021 and 2022.
- The California 2012–2019 series is a third-party compilation. Early zeros are reported values, and it holds permits only, not completions.
- TIGER 2024 boundaries match the ACS release. Joining 2026 parcels or boundaries will need a documented crosswalk.
- Gate 3 state and city series differ in definition (permits, applications, year built, report years), coverage (self-reported, partial) and date basis; several counts are `source_lead` news or vendor figures. Limits are listed per source in the Gate 3 method docs, and the compliance gaps in [DATA_COMPLIANCE.md](DATA_COMPLIANCE.md).

# Restricted, Sensitive, or Personal Data

The Madison permit report includes owner names and mailing addresses. The pipeline drops those columns at ingestion and never writes them to disk outside `data/raw/`, which is git-ignored. Street addresses are kept only for internal matching to parcels and are not published; outputs are counts by year and area. The committed samples in `data/sample/` replace owner and mailing fields with `[redacted]`, and a test checks this. The Pleasant Prairie report also holds owner names, mailing addresses and contractors; they stay in `data/raw/` and the loader drops them. Sun Prairie owner and contractor columns are never requested, and V12 parcel owner fields are never requested. Not every committed sample is masked yet: several state parcel samples and the HCD APR sample in `data/sample/` (and their tables in `docs/data-sources/`) still show site addresses with house numbers and full parcel numbers from public parcel layers; owner names are redacted in all of them. The fix is listed in [DATA_COMPLIANCE.md](DATA_COMPLIANCE.md). Gate 3 permit PDFs (De Pere, Howard, Watertown and others) hold owner, applicant or contractor names; they stay in `data/raw/` and only counts are committed. No restricted-use data is used: HUD/USPS vacancy data was excluded because access is limited to government and nonprofit users.

**Status: complete for Gate 2 review.** Rules for every candidate are also coded in [`reference/wi_adu_rules.csv`](../../reference/wi_adu_rules.csv) and summarized in [WI_ADU_RULES.md](WI_ADU_RULES.md).

## Recommendation (read this first)

**Strong candidate found: the Village of Howard (Brown County, about 20,000 people).** It meets both conditions today: record-level building permits are public for January 2012 to April 2026, every row has a parcel number that joins to Brown County parcels, and its zoning has no ADU allowance (R-1 is single-family only, and accessory buildings may not be living space). Fourteen restrictive years with parcel IDs is the longest clean window we found in a place that still prohibits ADUs.

### Why the ADU-equivalent rule is workable in each

None of these places labels ADUs, so the AQ-021 rule (a permit adding a dwelling unit on a parcel that already has one) does the work. It needs (a) a parcel id on the permit and (b) the parcel's prior dwelling count:

- Howard, Pleasant Prairie, Sun Prairie and Neenah permits carry county parcel numbers. Wisconsin V12 statewide parcels ([FeatureServer](https://services3.arcgis.com/n6uYoouQZW75n5WI/arcgis/rest/services/Wisconsin_Statewide_Parcels_DB/FeatureServer/0)) carry PARCELID/TAXPARCELID; a Neenah permit parcel `80612310000` matched V12 PARCELID exactly (1201 Green Acres Ln). Howard needs a `VH-` prefix to match Brown County ParcelID. Pleasant Prairie IDs need dash normalization (V12 format not yet verified). Sun Prairie uses the Dane 12-digit PARCELNO.
- V12 is a current snapshot, so "already has a dwelling" must come from either the year-built field, an earlier parcel vintage (V-series back to V1 2015), or a prior new-dwelling permit on the same parcel. Gate 2 permits work should test this on a sample.
- Expect very small counts (zero to a few per year). Duplex permits on a parcel that already holds a house, and teardown-rebuilds, must be separated by checking for a razing permit on the same parcel.

## Search method

1. Enumerated Wisconsin cities and villages of roughly 15,000–100,000 people (plus a few just under 15,000 where data looked strong), split into southeast, northeast/north/west, and south-central groups, plus Milwaukee as a historical option.
2. For each: looked for open data portals, ArcGIS REST services and arcgis.com items, Socrata (catalog search for Wisconsin building permits returned 0), monthly or annual permit reports with one row per permit, and public permit-system searches (Accela, eTRAKiT, SmartGov, Evolve, EnerGov, BS&A, OpenGov, iWorQ, Cloudpermit) that list permits by date range without login.
3. Rated: **GOOD** (record-level, multi-year, bulk, new units identifiable), **PARTIAL** (record-level but limited years or fields, or manual paging), **NONE** (login, lookup-by-number or address only, or summary counts), **UNKNOWN** (blocked or not found).
4. For GOOD and PARTIAL places, checked current and historical ADU rules.

## Milwaukee before 2025-08-02 as a historical restrictive period

Evaluated as an option; **weak, supplementary use only.**

- **Rules (verified):** ADUs were not a defined use before File 240999 (City Plan Commission staff report, 2025-04-07). New two-family dwellings were barred in RS districts unless legally established by 2002-10-01; second principal buildings barred in RS1–RS5 and RT1–RT3. File 240999 Substitute 4 passed 2025-07-15, effective 2025-08-02.
- **But** 44% of parcels are RT (two-family) districts, where an internal or attached second unit was already legal by right if lot area allowed. So a pre-2025 ADU-equivalent series must be restricted to RS parcels (52% of one-unit parcels).
- **Permits:** [data.milwaukee.gov buildingpermits](https://data.milwaukee.gov/api/3/action/package_show?id=buildingpermits) (CC-BY) covers late 2016 to 2026-06-15, 16,685 rows, but has no tax key, no description and no unit count, only a "Dwelling units impact" flag (Added/Gained 1,007 rows; blank 43%). Address match to MPROP found 97.9% of residential permits. Pre-2025 ADU-like cases exist (21 "detached garage + added unit" permits 2017–2025).
- **Better route:** annual MPROP snapshots 1975–2025 ([historical MPROP](https://data.milwaukee.gov/api/3/action/package_show?id=historical-master-property-file)) by TAXKEY: NR_UNITS changing from 1 to 2+ on RS-zoned parcels gives an ADU-equivalent series without permits.
- **Against:** big-city context, mostly older two-family stock, small counts (1–11 a year), and reportedly minimal uptake after the reform.

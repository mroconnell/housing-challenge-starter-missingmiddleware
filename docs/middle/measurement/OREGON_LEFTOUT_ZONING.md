# Oregon left-out cities: were duplexes already allowed before 2021?

Step 0 of the locked plan. These are zoning rules and dates only. No permit or production numbers were looked at or recorded.
Work done 2026-11-09, 19:34–19:58 UTC, by 4 parallel helpers. Detailed group notes are in scratchpad/olz/group_A.md to group_D.md.
**V** = read in the code or an official document (including Wayback copies of city files). **S** = secondary source (Zoneomics code mirror, news, search snippet).

## Result counts (out of 25 cities)
- Already allowed on most single-family lots before 2021: **3 of 25**
  - Molalla: V
  - Warrenton: V, R-10 zone only
  - Creswell: S, from 2020 only
- Unclear: **3 of 25** (Florence, North Bend, Madras, which leans allowed)
- Not allowed on most single-family lots: **19 of 25**
  - Some of these allowed duplexes on corner or large lots only (see table).
- Allowed duplexes in single-family zones early, before 2025-06-30: **8 of 25**
  - Hood River 2021, Molalla 2021, Talent 2021
  - Lincoln City 2022, Madras 2022, Sweet Home 2022, Astoria 2022
  - Monmouth 2023
  - Probably also Sheridan, Umatilla and Junction City in spring 2025.

## Flags for the study design
- **Lincoln City may not be left out.** Its 2022 staff report cites a PSU certified population of **10,067 people**, and the city treated itself as covered by HB 2001 (S). This conflicts with its place in the wider band.
- **Molalla acted as if HB 2001 applied** (council hearing 2021-06-23). Check its classification.
- **Hood River, Talent:** voluntarily allowed duplexes in spring 2021, at about the same time as the covered cities' deadline. They are effectively treated.
- **Sweet Home, Madras, Lincoln City, Astoria:** allowed duplexes in 2022. Their control period ends in 2022.
- **Monmouth:** allowed duplexes in 2023 (Ord 1408).
- **Warrenton R-10:** the current text says duplexes are allowed, but a 2025 staff memo snippet says middle housing is allowed only in medium/high zones. Check Ord 1234 (2020) or an archived pre-2020 copy.
- **Seaside, Milton-Freewater:** R-2 permits duplexes and holds much single-family land. How you define the main zone changes the verdict.
- **Lot-size conditions:** several "permitted" cases need larger lots. These are Scappoose (10,000 vs 6,000 sq ft), Lincoln City (8,000), Molalla (6,000 vs 5,000) and Madras (8,000 vs 7,500 on newer lots).

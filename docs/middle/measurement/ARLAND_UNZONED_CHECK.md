# Arland town (Barron Co., WI): is there a single-family limit to lift?

Checked 2026-11-10. V = read in the ordinance, statute, official document or official GIS. S = secondary source, or my reading/memory, not checked against the text.

## Short verdict

- The claim is **partly right**.
- **About 93% of Arland's land has no limit:** no zoning, so no single-family limit. (V)
- **About 7% of Arland's land is shoreland:** county zoning applies there in full. (V)
- **97% of that shoreland is zoned AG-2.** AG-2 allows **single-family homes only**, at a density of 2 homes per 40 acres, with a 17-acre minimum lot. (V)
- A further county-wide rule in zoned areas allows only **1 principal structure per lot** (17.15(1)). It applies in the shoreland. (V)
- So a reform allowing 2–6 homes per parcel would lift a real limit on about 7% of Arland's land. That only holds if the reform overrides county shoreland/general zoning districts.

Legal note (S, my reading): Wis. Stat. 59.692(1d)(a) bars county shoreland rules stricter than NR 115 on matters NR 115 regulates. Section 59.692(1d)(b) lets counties regulate matters NR 115 does not cover. NR 115 does not regulate land uses or homes per lot. So the AG-2 single-family rule most likely stands. Barron frames it as general zoning under § 59.971, now § 59.692 (17.10(1)(b)). I found no court ruling on this point.

Answer to the owner: **no county chapter outside Ch. 17/16 sets residential districts or limits homes per parcel.** The only homes-per-lot limits are in the general zoning districts. In Arland those districts reach only the shoreland.

### How landfills and other nuisance uses are controlled in an unzoned town

- **Inside Arland's shoreland:** county AG-2 lists "Private dumps and landfills" and junk or salvage yards as special exceptions only (17.37(2)(d)–(e)). These need Board of Adjustment approval. Unlisted uses are barred (17.12(3)). (V)
- **Outside the shoreland:** there are no county use districts. Landfills are controlled by the state:
  - DNR licensing and siting criteria under NR 500–538. (S: not read this session)
  - Wis. Stat. 289.33. Under 289.33(5)(a), siting is "a matter of statewide concern". (V)
  - Under 289.33(5)(c), a new facility is subject to **preexisting** local approvals. Under 289.33(5)(e), approvals adopted later do not apply unless a negotiation agreement says they do. (V)
  - Towns can join the negotiation and arbitration process by adopting a siting resolution within 60 days (289.33(6)(a)). (V)
- **Town powers:** a town with village powers can license or limit "offensive industries" and similar uses. Arland already does this for mining under § 66.0415 and §§ 60.10, 61.34. (V)
- **Courts:** common-law and statutory public nuisance actions (ch. 823) work without zoning. (S)

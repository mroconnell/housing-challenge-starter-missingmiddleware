# Maine and Vermont: 2–4 home projects in discretionary review

Researched 2026-11-10. Public web sources only. No permit counts or production numbers collected.
Tags: **V** = official record (minutes, decision, court opinion, statute, state guidance). **S** = news or secondary source.

## Bottom line

- **No clear denial found** in either state for a 2–4 home building on a single-family lot. This covers Maine 2024 to mid-2025 and Vermont 2023–2025.
- **Discretionary review was common.** It was usually approved, often with conditions.
- **Delays came mostly from neighbour appeals.** Boards rarely refused projects.
- **Vermont:** some towns still ran duplexes and triplexes through conditional use, site plan review, or DRB "discretionary permit" review after Act 47.
- **Maine:** planning-board review of 2–4 units was lawful during the study window. It was banned only by LD 1829, effective 24 Sept 2025, which is after the window.

### Out of scope, noted for context

- **Stowe, VT, 2025.** DRB **denied** a 22-unit PRD (4 duplexes + 2 seven-unit buildings). Reasons: village-green and settlement-pattern design standards. Environmental Division affirmed on 2 Feb 2026. The court noted that Stowe zoning makes duplexes **permitted** in all districts, while multi-family is **conditional**. Too large to count as a 2–4 home case. V. https://www.vtcourts.gov/sites/default/files/documents/Ampersand%20Properties%2C%20LLC%20PRD%20DRB%20Final%20Review%20App%2025ENV22%20OTR%20Merits.pdf
- **Portland, ME, March 2025.** 54-unit plan of 9 duplexes + 12 triplexes at 1 Hope Ave. Planning board **tabled** it over the developer's unpaid fines and soil concerns, and the project collapsed. A multi-building subdivision, not a single-lot case. S. https://www.pressherald.com/2025/08/13/portland-land-bank-commission-backs-bid-for-north-deering-land-once-eyed-for-condos/

## Table C: state law, guidance and courts on whether towns may use discretionary review

| Item | Date | What it says | Tag | Source |

| VT 24 V.S.A. §4412(1)(D), from Act 47 | eff. 1 Jul 2023 | Duplexes must be **"allowed"** wherever year-round homes are allowed, with dimensional standards no stricter than for a single-family home. | VT DHCD Act 47 preliminary summary | 28 Jun 2023 | Says 3–4 units in sewer/water areas must be a **"permitted (not conditional) use."** For duplexes it says only "must allow." | V | https://hardwickvt.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/DHCD-Planning-Act47-PreliminarySummary-v2.pdf |
| VLCT forum slides | Oct 2025 | For duplexes the statute "uses the term 'allow,' not 'permitted by right' or 'conditional use'." 3–4 units are "assumed to mean permitted by right."
